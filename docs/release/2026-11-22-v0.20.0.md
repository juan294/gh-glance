## Root cause: pre-existing slow-identity startup behavior, not a v0.20.0 regression

After the owner chose a root-cause investigation, the diagnostic copies of the candidate (`gh-glance-wt-cand-slow`) and of v0.19.4 (`gh-glance-wt-ctrl-slow`) received a per-call delay hook in the fixture `gh`. Each capture used the e2e settings: 80×24 and settle 7 s.

- Delaying `auth token` by 1.6 s, the budget probe by up to 5 s or the observer by up to 5 s still produced an Actions read in the window on both builds. Every delay of 2 s or more showed the transient "GitHub login unavailable" and `‖ Paused` on both builds.
- Delaying `api -i user`, the identity proof, by 3.5 s or more reproduced the hosted evidence exactly on both builds: the same five fixture calls, no Actions read, and a final `Watching next <1m`.
- Miss rate (no Actions read within 7 s) over 10 captures each: at 3.0 s, v0.20.0 2/10 and v0.19.4 2/10; at 3.5 s, v0.20.0 6/10 and v0.19.4 5/10. At 2.8 s both fetched.

Conclusion (VERIFIED by reproduction on both builds): when `gh api user` takes about 3 s or more on a cold start, the pane shows a false "GitHub login unavailable" and Paused. Its first Actions read can then slip to a later poll. v0.19.4 behaves the same at the same rates. The hosted macOS fixture's identity call was presumably that slow (INFERRED; the capture has no per-call timing). The exact scheduling path that defers the read is not traced: the control retry is 1 s (`governorControlRetryAt`), so the longer wait comes from elsewhere.
